A new boiler, fitted properly first time.

A-rated boilers fitted with a magnetic filter, a full system powerflush and Gas Safe certification, backed by extended warranties. Tidy, compliant and with minimal disruption.

Call 01952 300008

Why it matters

Fitted to manufacturer standards, so your warranty is protected.

As Baxi Approved and Ideal installers with Worcester Bosch training, every installation meets the manufacturer's standards, which protects your warranty.

We cover everything from a like-for-like swap to a full change of system, including moving from an old gravity-fed system to a combi.

  • A-rated, energy-efficient boilers
  • Magnetic filter fitted as standard
  • Full system powerflush
  • Gas Safe certification
  • Extended manufacturer warranties

How it works

From survey to switch-on.

  1. Site survey

    A Gas Safe engineer inspects your system, pipework and flue options and recommends the right A-rated boiler.

  2. Clear quote

    You get a clear, fixed price before any work starts. No hard sell.

  3. Installation

    We fit the boiler and magnetic filter, powerflush the system and keep the work area tidy.

  4. Handover

    We certify the work, show you how everything works and register your warranty.

Boiler replacement

Old boiler losing pressure or costing a fortune to run? We'll tell you honestly whether a repair or a replacement makes more sense, and quote clearly for both.

LPG boiler installations

Off the gas grid? We install LPG boilers for homes that rely on bottled or tank gas, with the same Gas Safe standards.

Questions

New boiler FAQs

Which boiler brands do you fit?
We're Baxi Approved and Ideal installers with Worcester Bosch training, and we work on other major makes including Vaillant, Glow-worm, Potterton, Vokera, Main, Alpha, ATAG and Intergas.
Will a new boiler affect my warranty?
Installing to manufacturer standards protects your warranty. Keeping up the annual service matters too, which is why many customers add a service plan.
How do I get a price?
Tell the assistant about your home and current boiler, or call 01952 300008. We'll arrange a survey and give you a clear price before any work starts.
