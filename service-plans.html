Questions

Service plan FAQs

What is a service plan and why do I need one?
A service plan gives you help if your boiler breaks down and affects your heating and hot water. It also spreads the cost of your annual boiler service, so you know we're just a call away.
How do I book my annual service?
Call us to book. On a plan, we'll also get in touch before your annual service is due so you can pick a time slot.
What components are covered?
The Silver plan covers your boiler and its internal components, radiators, radiator valves, heating controls (excluding smart controls) and internal gas pipework. Gold and Platinum add your vented hot water cylinder, pump and motorised valves.
Can I add electrical or plumbing cover?
Yes. Electrical cover and plumbing cover are £9.99 a month each on any plan up to Platinum. Platinum Extreme already includes electrics and plumbing call-out cover.
Are there any exclusions?
Unvented cylinders are not included in repair cover, and terms and conditions apply. Ask us for the full plan details before you sign up.
