Smell gas? Act now.

Open doors and windows, don't use switches or flames, turn off the gas at the meter if you safely can and leave the property. Then call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.

Common faults

Problems we fix every week.

  • No heating or hot water
  • Boiler losing pressure
  • Error and fault codes
  • Banging, gurgling or noisy pipes
  • Leaks from the boiler or pipework
  • Radiators not heating up
  • Ignition and pilot problems
  • Thermostat and control faults

Before you call

Two quick checks.

Check the pressure and the thermostat

Most combi boilers like a pressure between 1 and 1.5 bar, shown on the gauge on the front. And make sure the thermostat is turned up and the timer is on. If neither fixes it, get in touch.

LPG boilers

Off-grid LPG boiler repairs.

We repair LPG boilers for homes that rely on off-grid heating: low pressure, ignition problems, unusual noises or a complete breakdown.

Every repair is carried out safely and in line with current standards, with clear advice and honest recommendations.
