Our story

Local heating engineers you can rely on.

Every gas job is carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers. Our work is manufacturer-compliant and finished to a tidy, customer-focused standard.

Based in Wellington and covering the neighbouring towns, we know local homes well, from combi boilers to system and conventional installations, and we respond quickly to breakdowns and servicing requests.

Why choose us

What you can expect from us.

  1. Safety and compliance

    All gas work completed by Gas Safe registered engineers, with full safety paperwork and compliance checks on every job.

  2. Manufacturer-backed installers

    Baxi Approved and Ideal Installer credentials plus Worcester Bosch training mean installations meet manufacturer standards and protect your warranty.

  3. Proven customer satisfaction

    A 5.0 Google rating shows customers value our punctuality, clear communication and clean finish.

  4. Two decades of experience

    Over 20 years fitting, replacing and repairing boilers in Telford homes.

  5. Transparent pricing and cover

    A clear price before any work starts, and fixed-price service plans so you can plan ahead without surprises.

  6. Local, rapid response

    Based in Telford and covering neighbouring towns, so we can respond quickly to breakdowns and servicing requests.
